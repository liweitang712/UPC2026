Calibration of exact probabilities for centre entry and forward plane arrival on synthetic trials with resolved truth. Nominal decile bins include 1 in the final bin. Numbers below each panel are per-bin counts in model order. Bins below the locked minimum are omitted from the curves. Vertical bars are 95% Wilson intervals. The single-factor panels pool wind, drag, lift and sensor stress. Technical failures use all cases with resolved truth. Point abstentions use cases with a resolved forecast and a recorded point decision. The challenge sample is reported separately.
