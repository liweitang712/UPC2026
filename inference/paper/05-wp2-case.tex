\section{Prediction for the supplied kick}
The retained B0 reference gives an entry-conditioned modal crossing point
of approximately $(Y,Z)=(3.21,1.95)\,\mathrm m$.
For the 4,000 nominal public-case draws, the conditional median arrival
time is $0.37060\,\mathrm s$, leaving approximately
$0.27060\,\mathrm s$ after the second observation.
The marginal 95\% intervals, conditional on plane arrival, are
$[3.1467,3.2864]\,\mathrm m$ for $Y$,
$[1.8915,2.0245]\,\mathrm m$ for $Z$, and
$[0.36836,0.37284]\,\mathrm s$ for arrival time.
All nominal B0 draws reach the plane, enter the rectangle, and satisfy the
radius-clearance criterion. These values describe the sampled model
distribution. The independent public-case repetition leaves the modal grid
point unchanged.

B2 gives a center-entry probability of $0.7970$ and a radius-clearance
probability of $0.72475$. Its plane-conditioned marginal 95\% intervals are
$[1.6762,3.5670]\,\mathrm m$ for $Y$ and
$[1.0931,3.0045]\,\mathrm m$ for $Z$.
Its nominal entry mode is $(2.59,2.19)\,\mathrm m$, but increasing the
kernel bandwidth moves it by about $0.161\,\mathrm m$.
This sensitivity is recorded with the estimate.

The uncertainty intervals require a qualification from the independent
synthetic benchmark. The nominal 95\% joint regions cover only
31 of 437 eligible B0 crossings and 271 of 432 eligible B2 crossings in
the frozen blind evaluation. Both methods therefore substantially
undercover that benchmark distribution. The narrow B0 supplied-kick cloud
does not provide a calibrated estimate of total physical uncertainty.
Candidate aerodynamic refits, alternative ball curves, and observation
scenarios are reported as sensitivity analyses in the frozen release.
