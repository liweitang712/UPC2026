\section{Forecast for the supplied kick}\label{sec:case}

Under M2 with zero wind and independent 10\,mm Gaussian coordinate errors, the modeled probability of plane arrival is 100.0\%, the probability of center entry is 89.8\%, and the one-radius clearance probability is 83.0\%. The median plane arrival is \(0.398\,\second\) from the kick, about \(0.298\,\second\) after the second observation.

The 0.10\,m Gaussian-smoothed density conditional on entry peaks at \((2.83,1.91)\,\metre\). With an independent seed and 32,000 proposals the same smoothing gives \((2.83,1.89)\,\metre\). The two 0.05\,m-smoothed peaks differ by \(0.146\,\metre\), so we give the coarser peak as an approximate location and report its resolution. The joint plane-crossing region in Figure~\ref{fig:wp2-f2} includes possible wide crossings. Table~\ref{tab:case} also gives intervals conditional on entry.

Separate refits under 5, 10 and 20\,mm independent coordinate errors give M2 entry probabilities of approximately 99\%, 90\% and 74\%. Tightening the transverse prior standard deviation from 0.22 to 0.12 gives approximately 96\% under the 10\,mm assumption. These refits use the same frames. Their spread reflects sensitivity to observation and force assumptions that the data cannot settle.
